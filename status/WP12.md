- Notes / limits:
  - Pacing granularity is one viewer batch (32 packets, a few ms).
  - A first viewer starts at the oldest data, so with a 30-45 s startup burst its lead is the burst length, not exactly the delay. A provider burst on top of a full delay (30 s + 45 s) can exceed the 60 s ring; then the existing lag recovery applies.
  - The byte-rate fallback needs 2 s and 1 MiB of history; before that a stream with no PCR is unpaced.
  - Only `lib.rs` change outside the stream functions: a `viewer_lead_millis` field in a test literal, `SessionResponse` and `session_response` (diagnostics, as the brief says).
